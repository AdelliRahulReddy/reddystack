# ReddyStack public-site redesign — 2026-10-25

Built on branch `dev`. Not committed, not pushed, not deployed. /admin, the database and every price are untouched.

## Direction and why

- **Kept dark.** AGENTS.md says the site is dark-only, and changing that needs your word. The "light rate card" option from the review stays open for later.
- **Removed the AI-template look.** The old near-black ground with an acid-lime accent and violet glow is exactly what frontend-design lists as an "AI default". In its place:
  - one charcoal ground, one warm off-white ink, and **one marigold action colour** used only on actions (frontend-design: "spend your boldness in one place"; design-taste-frontend: "one ground, one ink, one accent").
  - Marigold is taken from the subject (Indian shopfronts and openings), not from a template palette.
- **The signature is an explainer, not decoration.** "A customer sees your ad → they message, call or fill a form → you get the enquiry" is animated in CSS and labelled "Example". hallmark calls this the product "doing its one job"; frontend-design says motion should explain what changed.
- **Plain words first** (DESIGN.md principle 1, frontend-design writing rules):
  - Homepage headline: "More customer enquiries, set up in 7 days."
  - "Enquiry Sprint" appears only as a small name next to the price.
  - "Meta campaign" became "Facebook & Instagram ads"; "async optimisation" became "we keep improving the ads for 7 days, with written updates".
- **One system everywhere:**
  - one type scale (display, h2, h3, body, small), Sora for headings and DM Sans for text, no monospace
  - one button style (filled, round), with any second action as a text link so no section shows two competing buttons
  - one card style (tone step, no borders or shadows) and one section rhythm
  - (ui-ux-pro-max: a consistent, minimal style; design-system: tokens; ui-styling: Tailwind tokens.)
- **Removed:**
  - scrambling labels, eyebrows and mono labels
  - coloured accent words, the glass pill menu, glow and grid backgrounds
  - custom cursor, magnetic buttons, smooth scrolling, GSAP scroll scenes, page transitions
  - (hallmark floor rules 3, 9, 10 and 11; design-taste-frontend house rules; emil-design-eng's "should this animate at all?")
- **Lighter by deletion** (ponytail, next-bundle-optimizer): no new dependencies were added.

## Light conversion (owner decision, 2026-10-25)

Same system, converted to light. Layout, plain words, the explainer, the labelled examples, the menu, footer, breadcrumbs, country pages, phone bar and enquiry step are unchanged. Not committed, not deployed.

### New light tokens and why

| Role | Colour | Contrast | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background | bone `#f6f4ef` | — | A low-colour warm stone, not yellow cream (design-taste-frontend: warmth from stone or bone, avoid the cream look) |
| Text | ink `#1c1915` | 16:1 | Warm near-black, never pure black |
| Secondary text | `#5c564c` | 6.7:1 | Passes 4.5:1 with room for daylight on a phone |
| Cards and panels | white `#ffffff` + edge `#ded7ca` + soft shadow | — | On light, a card needs an edge or it reads as loose text (your brief; this overrides the skill's tone-only rule) |
| Button fill | marigold `#f2b13a` with ink `#1a1306` on it | 9.6:1 | Keeps the marigold identity |
| Accent text and links | dark amber `#8a5a00` | 5.5:1 | Marigold as small text on white would be about 1.8:1, so text uses this darker shade |
| Form field borders | `#8f8677` | 3.3:1 | Meets the 3:1 rule for form-field edges |
| Errors | `#b42318` | about 6:1 | Readable red on light |

Everything stays scoped to the public site, so /admin keeps its dark look exactly. The shared Button's hover colour now comes from a public-only setting; admin falls back to its old behaviour.

## Homepage improvement run (2026-10-26)

Based on direct inspection of the live light site. Branch `dev`, not committed, not deployed.

### The new hero, and why

- **It sells the outcome, not one plan.** The headline stays "More customer enquiries, set up in 7 days", with one support line and one action ("Get customer enquiries", which goes to the contact page). The hero shows no price and no plan name.
- **The main visual is a tap-through enquiry journey.** The three steps sit beside a phone mock:
  1. A customer sees your ad.
  2. They message, call or fill a form.
  3. The enquiry reaches your phone.

  Tapping a step changes the phone screen with a short eased fade and lift (opacity and movement only). The phone keeps a fixed size, so nothing jumps.
- **Autoplay runs once.** It steps through 1 → 2 → 3, then stops. It stops for good the moment the visitor touches, clicks or focuses it.
- **Reduced motion:** the whole journey shows as a still picture of all three screens. No JavaScript is needed for this.
- **Rahul is secondary:** a small photo and the line "answers every enquiry himself" under the button.
- **Why** (frontend-design and design-taste-frontend): the hero opens with the product doing its one job, and the "one live thing" the visitor can try is the journey itself. emil-design-eng: motion answers a tap, eases in about 260ms, and never loops aggressively.
