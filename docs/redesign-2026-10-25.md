# ReddyStack public-site redesign — 2026-10-25

Built on branch `dev`. Not committed, not pushed, not deployed. /admin, the database and every price are untouched.

## Direction and why

- **Kept dark.** AGENTS.md says the site is dark-only, and changing that needs your word. The "light rate card" option from the review stays open for later.
- **Removed the AI-template look.** The old near-black ground with an acid-lime accent and violet glow is exactly what frontend-design lists as an "AI default". In its place:
  - one charcoal ground, one warm off-white ink, and **one marigold action colour** used only on actions (frontend-design: "spend your boldness in one place"; design-taste-frontend: "one ground, one ink, one accent").
  - Marigold is taken from the subject (Indian shopfronts and openings), not from a template palette.
- **The signature is an explainer, not decoration.** "A customer sees your ad → they message, call or fill a form → you get the enquiry" is animated in CSS and labelled "Example". hallmark calls this the product "doing its one job"; frontend-design says motion should explain what changed.
- **Plain words first** (DESIGN.md principle 1, frontend-design writing rules):
  - Homepage headline: "More customer enquiries, set up in 7 days."
  - "Enquiry Sprint" appears only as a small name next to the price.
  - "Meta campaign" became "Facebook & Instagram ads"; "async optimisation" became "we keep improving the ads for 7 days, with written updates".
- **One system everywhere:**
  - one type scale (display, h2, h3, body, small), Sora for headings and DM Sans for text, no monospace
  - one button style (filled, round), with any second action as a text link so no section shows two competing buttons
  - one card style (tone step, no borders or shadows) and one section rhythm
  - (ui-ux-pro-max: a consistent, minimal style; design-system: tokens; ui-styling: Tailwind tokens.)
- **Removed:**
  - scrambling labels, eyebrows and mono labels
  - coloured accent words, the glass pill menu, glow and grid backgrounds
  - custom cursor, magnetic buttons, smooth scrolling, GSAP scroll scenes, page transitions
  - (hallmark floor rules 3, 9, 10 and 11; design-taste-frontend house rules; emil-design-eng's "should this animate at all?")
- **Lighter by deletion** (ponytail, next-bundle-optimizer): no new dependencies were added.
